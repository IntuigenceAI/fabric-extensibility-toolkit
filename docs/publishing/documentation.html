Overview

IntuigenceAI Industrial Workers is a Microsoft Fabric workload published by IntuigenceAI. It brings AI-powered Synthetic Engineers directly into your Fabric environment, enabling industrial teams to analyze P&ID diagrams, query plant documents, and get expert-level insights — all without leaving Fabric.

🤖

AI Synthetic Engineers

Deploy PhD-level AI engineers trained on industrial knowledge directly inside your workspace.

🗺

P&ID Analysis

Analyze piping & instrumentation diagrams and understand equipment connections automatically.

🗃

Knowledge Graph Catalog

Upload and index P&IDs, timeseries data, and documents into a structured knowledge graph.

📎

Grounded Responses

Every answer is backed by source citations from your own documents and plant data.

Preview Notice: This workload is currently in preview. Features and UI may change. Contact your admin to configure the workload for your organization.

How to Start the Workload

After logging in to Microsoft Fabric, navigate to Workloads in the left sidebar to find the IntuigenceAI workload.

1

Open the Workloads page

In the left sidebar, click Workloads. Locate IntuigenceAI (preview) and click Learn more.

2

Open the workload

Click Open in the "Get Started" panel. Select your workspace with a valid Fabric capacity.

3

Create a Data Catalog

Create a Data Catalog item to store and index your plant files (P&IDs, documents, timeseries).

4

Create an Intelligent Board

Create an Intelligent Board item and link it to your Data Catalog to start your first Agent Session.

Admin configuration required: The workload must be enabled by your Fabric administrator. If you see "Contact your admin to configure this workload", reach out to your IT or Fabric admin.

Supported file types

  • 1
    P&ID — Piping & Instrumentation Diagrams

    Upload PDF or image-format P&ID sheets. The AI will automatically extract equipment tags, line numbers, and connections.

  • 2
    Timeseries — Operational Data (Excel / CSV)

    Upload timeseries data exported from your historian or process control system. Supports .xlsx and .csv formats.

  • 3
    Document — General Documents

    Upload equipment manuals, SOPs, maintenance records, and other non-P&ID PDFs. PAM will cite these in its answers.

File status

  • ✓
    Success

    The file has been fully indexed and is available to PAM for querying and citation.

  • ⏳
    In progress

    The file is still being processed. Large or complex P&IDs may take additional time. Refresh the page to check the latest status.

Analyzing P&ID Diagrams

P&ID files tagged as P&ID open in a dedicated diagram viewer with pan and zoom controls. The AI engine automatically parses the diagram to extract equipment tags, instrument loops, and pipe connections for use by PAM.

  • What are the upstream and downstream connections of equipment tag HX-301?
  • Which instruments are on line 6"-CS-3021?
  • List all pressure indicators on this P&ID sheet.

AI Plant Support Engineer (PAM)

PAM is the primary AI worker available inside the Intelligent Board. PAM has access to your indexed Data Catalog and answers complex operational questions with grounded citations.

What you can ask PAM

You
What is the status of heat exchanger HX-301?
PAM
Based on the indexed P&ID and equipment documentation, HX-301 is rated for shell-side service at 150 psig / 300°F. [Source: P&ID Sheet 12-A, Rev 4 · Equipment Manual HX-301]
You
Show upstream and downstream connections for HX-301.
PAM
HX-301 receives feed from pump P-210 via line 6"-CS-3021 and discharges to separator V-402 via 4"-CS-3022. [Source: P&ID Sheet 12-A, Rev 4]

Grounded Responses & Source Citations

Every answer PAM gives is grounded — traceable back to your own industrial data. Each response includes citations in the format:

[Source: P&ID Sheet 07-B, Rev 2 · Equipment Manual CP-500X]

Click on citation references within the Intelligent Board to jump directly to the source document or P&ID sheet in the Document Viewer.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • ?
    Why do I see "Contact your admin to configure this workload"?

    The workload must be explicitly enabled by a Fabric administrator in your organization's Admin settings.

  • ?
    How long does indexing take?

    Small documents index within seconds. Large P&ID PDFs or multi-sheet files may show In progress for several minutes. Refresh the Data Catalog to check the latest status.

  • ?
    Can I upload image-format P&IDs?

    Yes. .png, .jpg, and .pdf are all supported for P&ID files.

  • ?
    What does "preview" mean?

    The workload is functional but subject to change before general availability. It is covered by IntuigenceAI's preview terms of use.
