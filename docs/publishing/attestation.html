Security / Compliance Requirements

1. Security General

Protection of customer data and metadata is of paramount importance. Workloads must go through a security review and assessment. Vendor attests that the security review and assessment was completed and will be periodically performed as enhancements and changes are made.

IntuigenceAI maintains a formal security program including SOC 2 Type I compliance. Security reviews are conducted as part of the company's security governance processes. Controls include:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+)
  • Encryption at rest (AES-256)
  • Role-based access control
  • Continuous monitoring of infrastructure and application environments
2. Privacy

Partners that build workloads also have a responsibility to protect that data when they access it. Every workload goes through a privacy assessment and a privacy review. Vendor attests that privacy review was completed and is periodically performed as enhancements and changes are made.

Extra Requirements:

  • [x] Publisher attests that only essential HTTP-only cookies are used by the Workload and only after positively authenticating the user
  • [x] Publisher attests that it is not using or relying on third-party cookies as part of their solution
  • [x] Publisher attests to obtaining any Microsoft Entra token using the JavaScript APIs provided by the Fabric Workload Client SDK
IntuigenceAI performs privacy reviews as part of its secure SDLC and SOC 2 Type I compliance program. Privacy impact is assessed during design and before release of material changes. Customer data handling is governed by the IntuigenceAI Privacy Policy and Data Processing Agreement, including limitations on data retention, access, and use. For the Microsoft Fabric workload, IntuigenceAI relies on identity provided by Microsoft Entra and does not persist the user's name, email address, or phone number within the workload.
3. Data Residency

Microsoft Fabric is making an Enterprise Promise around data not leaving the geography of the tenant for stored data and data in transit. As a workload in Fabric, customers need to be aware of your commitments to Data Residency.

IntuigenceAI stores and processes customer content data in IntuigenceAI‑managed Azure resources in the us‑west2 region and, where configured, in the customer's own Fabric tenant environment. Customer content may therefore leave the tenant's geography depending on the customer's region and configuration. Operational telemetry and logs may be processed in additional regions but do not include customer content. For details, see https://intuigence.ai/privacy.
4. Compliance

The publisher attests to the following security, data, and compliance regulations and standards.

IntuigenceAI maintains SOC 2 Type I compliance and operates under a formal security and compliance program. Security controls include:

  • Encryption of data at rest and in transit
  • Role-based access control
  • Secure cloud infrastructure management
  • Continuous monitoring of systems

Security controls are documented and tracked through the company's governance, risk, and compliance platform.
